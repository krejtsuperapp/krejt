## What we collect

We collect only what we need to deliver the service you ask for.

- Your phone number. It is the only way to sign in: we send a code by SMS and check it.
- Your name, and — if you provide them yourself — your email address and profile photo.
- Your location, only while you use the app and only for the services that need it: the pickup point, tracking a ride or an order, finding places near you. We do not track your location when the app is closed.
- The addresses you save yourself (home, work, others).
- Your history of rides, orders, parcels and service requests: what, when, where, what it cost and how it was paid.
- The messages you exchange with a driver, courier or support, and any safety reports you send.
- Technical details about your device: model, operating system, app version and the identifier used for notifications.

We do not store card numbers. When card payment opens, card details go straight to the payment provider and never pass through our servers.

## Why we process it

- To deliver the service: to find a driver or courier, to send your order to the merchant, to calculate the price and to keep your wallet balance.
- For your safety and the driver's: account verification, abuse detection, handling reports.
- For support: a complaint about a ride cannot be resolved without the ride.
- To meet the legal and tax obligations we have as an operator.

## Who we share it with

The driver, courier, merchant or professional sees only what the job requires: your name, the pickup and drop-off points, and a way to reach you. Your real number is not handed over when contact happens through the app.

Beyond them, your data is entrusted only to the providers that keep the service running:

- Amazon Web Services — storage and processing, in data centres in Frankfurt, Germany.
- Infobip — delivery of the SMS sign-in code.
- Google Firebase — delivery of notifications to your device.
- Mapbox — maps, address search and route calculation.

We do not sell your data to anyone and we do not hand it over for advertising.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account and history for as long as the account is open. After you delete your account, personal data is removed; only the financial records the law requires us to keep remain, separated from your identity wherever that is possible. Support messages are kept for as long as the case needs.

## Your rights

- View and change your profile data inside the app, under Account.
- Delete your account inside the app. Deletion is immediate and irreversible; your wallet must be empty first.
- Request a copy of your data, or complain about how we process it, by writing to {{email}}.

## Children

KREJT is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data about anyone younger; if we learn that we have, we delete it.

## Changes

When this policy changes, the date above changes with it, and for changes that genuinely affect you we give notice inside the app before they take effect.
